# Business Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project was completed as part of my **Data Analytics Internshi
p at Veda Technology**.

The project focuses on analyzing business performance using the **Sample Superstore dataset** and creating an interactive **Power BI dashboard** to identify key business trends, performance indicators, and actionable insights.

## Objectives

* Analyze overall business performance using key KPIs
* Calculate and interpret revenue, profit, profit margin, customer count, and growth
* Compare monthly and yearly performance
* Analyze regional performance
* Analyze product/category performance
* Identify business problems and provide recommendations

## Tools Used

* Power BI
* DAX
* Sample Superstore Dataset

## Key KPIs

The dashboard includes:

* Total Revenue
* Total Profit
* Profit Margin %
* Previous Year Performance
* Customer Count
* Growth %

## Analysis Performed

### Regional Performance

Sales and profit were analyzed across different regions to identify stronger and weaker performing areas.

### Product Performance

Product categories were compared based on sales and profit to identify high-performing and lower-profit categories.

### Time-Based Analysis

Monthly sales and profit trends were analyzed to understand changes in business performance over time.

## Business Insights

1. **Furniture Profitability:** Furniture shows lower profitability despite significant sales. Shipping costs and discount levels should be reviewed to improve margins.

2. **Technology Performance:** Technology contributes strongly to overall profit. Bundling slower-moving products with popular technology products may help increase sales.

3. **Regional Performance:** Regional sales and profit differences can be used to identify areas requiring targeted marketing or discount optimization.

4. **Customer Growth vs Revenue:** Customer growth can be supported by loyalty initiatives and strategies aimed at increasing average order value.

5. **Discount Management:** Excessive discounting can affect profitability, so discount policies should be reviewed to protect margins.

## Dashboard

The Power BI dashboard provides an interactive view of the business KPIs, monthly trends, regional performance, product performance, and business recommendations.
